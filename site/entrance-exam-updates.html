JEE, NEET and Entrance Exam Updates

Entrance examinations can have separate notices for registration, correction windows, city intimation, admit cards, answer keys, scorecards and counselling. Verify the stage and year before following a notice.

Verification note: GoResultHub is an independent directory, not a government website. Use listings to discover notices; verify dates, eligibility, deadlines and candidate-specific details on the issuing authority's official website.

JEE Main

Check the National Testing Agency's official JEE Main pages for information bulletins, application corrections, examination notices, answer keys and scorecards. Session-wise notices may differ.
