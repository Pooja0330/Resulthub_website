What to include in a correction request

Please share the exam or recruitment name, the GoResultHub page URL, the official authority URL and a short description of the problem. If possible, include the date you noticed the issue.

Safety and response

Never send passwords, one-time passwords, application login details, full identity documents or payment information. GoResultHub cannot retrieve candidate-specific results or change an application submitted to an examination authority. For account, payment or application problems, contact the authority operating the official portal.

Messages are reviewed as time permits. Sending a correction does not guarantee that a listing will be changed; updates are checked against the relevant official source.
