Editorial Policy

Use official exam authorities as the primary source for dates, results and instructions. Clearly distinguish confirmed declarations from estimates. Record when each listing was checked, correct broken links promptly and never invent result statuses. Review links periodically. Add automated monitoring only where the source permits it, and require human verification for ambiguous announcements.

Source selection

Official government departments, examination authorities, recruitment bodies, boards and universities are the primary sources for listings. Third-party result aggregators are not treated as the authority for confirming a result or deadline.

Classification and publication

Automated checks identify potentially relevant pages using source lists and exam-related terms. Listings are grouped by update type, such as result, admit card, answer key, recruitment notice or exam schedule. Automated classification can be imperfect, so users should open and verify the original notice.

Dates and corrections

Where available, the original publication date is distinguished from the time GoResultHub detected a page. We aim to correct broken links, duplicate listings and classification errors when they are reported or found during review. A listing may be removed or updated if the official source changes.

Independence

GoResultHub is an independent directory, not a government website and not affiliated with the authorities whose public pages it links to. We do not claim to publish official results or make candidate-specific decisions.
