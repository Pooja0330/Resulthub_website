Important disclaimer

GoResultHub helps students find exam notifications, results, admit cards, answer keys and recruitment updates, with links to the relevant official government, examination board, university and recruitment websites.

Use the “Visit Official Website” link on an exam listing to open the relevant authority’s website for its published notice or result. Availability and instructions are managed by that authority.

Candidate-specific result checking is handled by the examination authority through its official website. GoResultHub does not ask students to enter their examination passwords or OTPs.
