About ResultHub

ResultHub is an independent website that tells students when an exam result is declared and sends them to the official result page. We cover national exams (UPSC, SSC, IBPS, RRB, NTA, CA, CS), state commissions, entrance exams such as NEET and GATE, and abroad exams such as GRE, GMAT, TOEFL and IELTS.

How we work: we read the official notice, post the update with the date and a short note, and link to the official website. We do not host results, store student data or ask for login details. Our posts are for information only, so please confirm every detail on the official website.

Privacy Policy

  • What we collect: ResultHub does not ask for your name, roll number, registration number or password, and has no sign-up or login. Your theme choice (light or dark) is applied only in your browser.
  • Hosting logs: Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and to measure traffic.
  • Advertising and cookies: If we show ads such as Google AdSense, Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can manage this in your Google ad settings.
  • External links: We link to official websites and to our Telegram channel. Those sites have their own privacy policies and we are not responsible for them.
  • Changes: We may update this policy, and the latest version is always on this page.
