Privacy policy

GoResultHub is an independent directory of exam notifications and result links. It does not need your exam roll number, application number, password, OTP or date of birth to browse public listings. Enter candidate-specific details only on the relevant official authority website.

Advertising and affiliate links

If advertising is enabled, third-party providers such as Google AdSense may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, limit, measure or personalise ads, subject to the provider's settings and your location. Advertisements will be identified as such. If partner or affiliate links are enabled, they will be labelled; GoResultHub may receive a commission when a qualifying action occurs, without increasing the price to you.

Analytics

Analytics tools may be enabled later to understand aggregate visits and traffic sources. This policy and any required consent controls must be updated before analytics or advertising services are activated.

External websites

Links to examination authorities, advertisers and partners lead to websites governed by their own privacy policies. Review those policies before submitting information. GoResultHub does not collect candidate credentials for result checking.

Your choices and contact

You can adjust cookie and privacy controls offered by your browser or any advertising provider. For privacy questions, contact GoResultHub support. This policy should be reviewed for the services actually enabled and the laws that apply to your visitors.
